# PurpleBook-Doom

A variant of the Purple Book generator. The normal Purple Book stays as it is. This variant is the book for the Doom install.

Generated by `Generator/SCRIPT-PurpleBookDoom.py`. Output is `PurpleBook-Doom/` beside the generator, and a copy is placed in the Doom snapshot at `00_Support/DoomSurvival/Mods/AGF-PurpleBook-Doom`.

## What changes

- No armor pages. No Unlocks page.
- Learnings weapons are two columns. The right column is Chaingun, Rocket Launcher, Plasma Rifle, then Medical under Plasma Rifle. Cooking is the full-width row under both.
- Weapon and Medical unlock tiles fill the right side of each section and sit flush with its top, bottom, and right border. The level chip is the full width of each tile, with space beside the icon. The title, level, and xp bar sit in the left side and are vertically centered. Every xp bar is the same width. `/max` shares the line with the larger current level.
- Cooking keeps that same header above its xp bar. Its unlock tiles span the row under that header.
- Ammo is the third tab. Each pouch shows the current stack size and the pouch level out of 40.
- The Books tab uses the original header: Learnings and Books on the right, brown Overview on the left, cover strip, overview squares, and a zoomed list. The last book of a series is the completion row (check or lock), not a numbered volume. Cover art uses the item CustomIcon when one is set.
- Each skill header is icon, title, and a right-aligned level, then a taller orange bar with the XP fraction in white.
- Each craft tile has a small level chip inside the box. Icons stack two high.
- Books is the second tab. One row per series, green once that volume is read.

## What a skill is

Reading a book adds XP to one Learn by Doing skill. The skill level is what unlocks crafting. There is no magazine read-count.

Skills in the Doom install, each max 50:

- Cooking (`lbu_Cooking`)
- Medical (`lbu_Medical`)
- Gunslinger (`lbu_Gunslinger`)
- Archery (`lbu_Archery`)
- Robotics (`lbu_Robotics`)
- Boomstick (`lbu_Boomstick`)
- Machine Gunner (`lbu_MachineGunner`)
- Demolition (`lbu_Demolition`)
- Dead Eye (`lbu_DeadEye`)

Books add to `lbu_*_XP`. Crafts use `UnlockedBy` set to that perk.

## Learnings layout

See What changes. The craft level sits in a small chip inside the tile, and the tile turns green once the skill has reached that level.
