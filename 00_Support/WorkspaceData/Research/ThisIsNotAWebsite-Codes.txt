HOW TYPING WORKS
The box accepts any text. Almost all of it does nothing.
The site lowercases what you type, then keeps only letters, digits, ?, infinity, and the section sign.
Spaces and other punctuation are ignored, so "BYE GOLD!" and "byegold" are the same code.
On the normal desk, the letters "wip" are deleted wherever they appear.
Type BACKSTAGE to switch desks. After that, the same phrase is sent with "wip" stuck on the front, so it can do something different.
Anything shorter than 2 characters after that cleanup is rejected on the page.
Unknown phrases flash an X. The server does not publish its master list.
