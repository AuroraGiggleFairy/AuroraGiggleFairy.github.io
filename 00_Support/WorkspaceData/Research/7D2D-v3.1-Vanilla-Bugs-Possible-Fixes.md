# 7D2D v3.1 — Vanilla bugs / AGF mitigations

**Game:** v3.1 (same class of bugs on v2.x). **AGF did not cause these.** VET only adds tags.  
**Started:** 2026-08-22. Living note — add a row + a short section when a new bug is picked.

TFP will not hotfix this list away. Crawl, grid pathing, cheap melee, and interpolation are still the engine. We can still make fights **fair** if we only stop damage that should not have landed, and pull zombies back out of walls. We make the game worse if we rewrite crawl, pathing, or netcode.

## v1 To Do — what we can improve

One new NoEAC combat helper. Each item is a toggle. Leave zombie reach numbers as vanilla.

**In v1** (same helper, each item a toggle)

1. **Closed door / wall / floor / hatch / bars** — if that block is still in the way (or they are stuck inside it), their punch does not hurt you. Open doors, open hatches, and swing-through gaps stay normal. Grass is not cover.
2. **Invisible slap** — if they never swung, you take no punch damage.
3. **You already backed up** — when the punch would land, check distance again. If you are out of reach, it misses.
4. **Pull them out** — after a short wait, move them up out of the floor, or back to the outside of the door/wall they glitched through (including horde-night pile-through).
5. **Face-hit pain anim** — hands-on-face or legs-buckle must not walk them toward you faster than not hitting them. They do not punch during that anim. Not PainResist.
6. **Grass / plant does not eat your swing (B07)** — if they are on **this swing’s ray** and only a loose plant is in front, you hit them. Empty-field harvest stays vanilla. Do not strip plant `Collide` in XML. Closed doors still stop you. Wood spikes are not plants.
7. **Get-up empty hitbox (B08)** — do **not** turn their colliders back on. If your **melee or ranged** (gun / bow / crossbow) goes through chest/pelvis while they are standing up, and no closed door/wall/hatch is in the way, it counts. Explosions / vomit stay vanilla.

**How we test (not a gameplay fix):** **Test overlay — draw their punch range.** Off unless we turn it on. Shows the real Range + Sphere bubble (and the punch line) so we can see items 1–4 in a T5 and on horde night. Does not change damage.

**Not this helper** (later or never)

- Making them walk straight (path smoothing) — separate project.
- Shortening how far the files say they can reach.
- Turning off crouch/crawl.
- Changing cop vomit, explosions, or other specials.
- Letting you hit *them* through a closed door.
- Changing `PainResistPerHit` (that’s the no-anim / “pain doesn’t slow them” meter).
- Turning their walking capsule back on during ragdoll / get-up (launches them).

**Done when:** a T5 and a blood moon feel fair — no cheap hits through closed walls, no living in the floor, hatch fights still work, vents still work, grass does not eat your swing, get-up is still hittable.

## Why TFP left it

| Constraint | What it causes |
|---|---|
| 1-block crawl needs a **smaller AABB** (A20, then v2.0 crawl pass) | Voxel “which side?” picks the floor/door more often. Crowd shove (B05) finishes the job. A never-clip fix breaks vents/sleepers they just shipped. |
| Horde-night CPU | Melee is **origin + Range + Sphere**, not a fist. They bumped `meleeHandMaster` to 1.65 after a code change (XML comment: not “clientside melee”). |
| Fully destructible world | Pathing is a **1 m grid**, not a navmesh. Zigzag is the cost. Smoothing crashed dedicated servers in mods. |
| Server auth + interpolation | You see the zombie late and yourself early. Real WYSIWYG is rewind = cheat surface with EAC on. |
| New crouch/crawl anims | Zero-frame snaps; QA won’t catch the 8th T5 hatch pile. |
| Face-hit pain anim root motion | Hands-on-face / legs-buckle can walk them **in**. Separate from PainResistPerHit (at 1, pain no longer slows them; Frantic_Dan: no-anim hits). |
| Grass/crops `Collide="melee"` only | You can walk and they can punch through; **your** ray stops. Harvest needs that bit. |
| Ragdoll turns off zombie `PhysicsTransform` | Walking capsule stays off until late get-up. Melee and ranged both miss air. Capsule-on-during-ragdoll launches them. |
| Consoles + EAC + POIs | Our weekend DLL ≠ their certified `EntityMoveHelper` change. PC mods already exist → low pressure. |

They have patched pieces (head offset, crouch rules, “full slide-through”). The **class** stays because the systems that cause it are still in use.

## B01 — In the floor/wall, still attacking

**See:** chest-up in the floor, or inside a wall; they walk and hit; you need a clipping limb to hit back. Piles, stairs, doors, crouch. Thin doors/plates → **B02**.

**Cause:** Crawl shrank the box. Most of the box inside a solid → resolved to the wrong side. Their melee uses origin/range; yours needs a visible collider.

**Not XML.** Glitch Helper *kills* leftover quest zeds. Off-world mods only catch invalid Y.

**Build:** same rules as B02. Check nearby zombies a few times a second. If they’re in the floor or a wall is between you, **their punch does no damage**. If they’re still stuck after about a second, **move them up** into open air and stand them up if there’s room. Don’t turn off crouch. Don’t throw a zombie who is actually crawling a 1-block vent.

**Test:** open wilderness (nobody teleports for no reason); vent crawl still works; T5 stairs = no cheap hits, then they pop out; you still can’t hit them through the floor above unless we add that later.

**Refs:** [floor/wall clip](https://community.thefunpimps.com/threads/zs-glitching-through-walls-and-floors.44739/) · [instant crouch](https://steamcommunity.com/app/251570/discussions/1/3196990234723313972/)

## B02 — Hits through a closed door / thin barrier

**See:** closed door, hatch, plate, bars; they hug or clip through; you get hit; your swing hits the door.

**Cause:** Blocks already say what melee may pass (`Collide` → `Block.BlockingType`). Player swings honor `IsCollideMelee`. Zombie melee does **not** — range from origin. Thin slabs fail “which side?” more than cubes. If they’re already on your side, LOS is air → also need “origin inside the solid.”

`PassThroughDamage` is leftover **block** damage into the downgrade. Not “punch through this door.”

| Barrier | Typical `Collide` | They should melee through? |
|---|---|---|
| Closed door / hatch, cube wall/floor, plate | melee + movement (or all bits) | **No** |
| Iron bars (`ironBarsCentered`) | `movement,melee,rocket` | **No** (you can still shoot) |
| Open door / hatch, pole / gap | opening is air | **Yes** |
| Crops / grass | `melee` only | **Yes** (not cover — don’t god-mode fields). Your swing still *stops* on that same `melee` bit — that’s **B07** |

**Build (same code as B01):** **their punch does no damage** if (1) a line from their chest to you hits a block the game already marks as blocking melee, and that block is not open, or (2) their chest is inside that block. Then **push them back** to their side of the door (not up). Use the same kind of check your own swing uses for doors.

**Test:** closed wood door / closed hatch = they don’t chip you; opened = they can hit; bars = they don’t punch through, you can still shoot; grass = they still hit; if they popped all the way through, no damage until they’re pushed back.

**Refs:** [door clip](https://community.thefunpimps.com/threads/clipping-issues.27838/)

## B03 — “Insane” reach (open air)

**See:** slap from empty air; worse when backpedaling or non-host; crouch/hug snap; occasional **hit with no swing** after stun-mash. Door/floor between you = B01/B02, not this.

**Cause (stacked):**

1. **XML is real, from the entity, not the fist.** Body + your collider ≈ a step of air can still be legal.
2. Hit tick ≠ fist-connect. Commit at start, apply later → you already left and still get hit.
3. Crouch/hug snaps the mesh; origin/`Range` stay.
4. Server positions vs interpolated client (zombie behind, you ahead).
5. Ghost apply with no attack state (interrupt order). Not Range XML.

Walkers are **not** 3 m in XML. Felt 2.5–3 m = 1.6 + sphere + body + timing + sync + crouch + sometimes B02.

| Item | Range (m) | Sphere | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `meleeHandMaster` | 1.65 | 0.1 | TFP: not clientside melee. `Block_range` **2.5** = blocks, not slaps |
| Walker `…Zombie01` | 1.6 | 0.1 | |
| Short / burning | 1.4 | burning **0.4** | |
| Crawler `…Zombie02` | 1.3 | 0.1 | Hardcoded swap |
| Cop / Rancher / Chuck | 1.75 | 0.1 | |
| Mutated | 1.7 | 0.1 | |
| Boar | 2.2 | 0.1 | |
| Bear / z-bear | 3.1 | 0.3 | |
| Dire wolf / mountain lion | 2.2 | | |
| HazMat / Worker | 1.65 inherit | 0.1 | |
| Player spear | 2.6 / 3.6 | | Honest checks: you still outrange walkers |

XML Range nerf ([Kitsune](https://github.com/Kitsune-Den/KitsuneZombieReach)) makes the bubble **smaller**, not honest. Testers: walker 2.0+ m wrecks clubs. Leave numbers vanilla until 1+2 prove we need a “Fair” preset.

**Build:** same punch-damage check as B02, then: re-measure distance when the hit would land (use hand `Range` + `Sphere`, not the 2.5 m *block* number); **no damage if they didn’t swing**. Draw debug bubbles. Leave vomit/etc. alone. Bears at 3.1 m stay long — that’s the number in the files, not a glitch.

**Test:** 1.2 m stand still = hit; windup then sprint out = no hit; pingy dedicated uses **server** pos; mash = no ghost; door = B02; vomit unchanged.

**Refs:** [reach thread](https://community.thefunpimps.com/threads/zombie-melee-attack-reach.45241/) · [Range as difficulty](https://steamcommunity.com/app/251570/discussions/0/3712684644825014219/)

## B04 — Voxel sidestep (path smoothing)

**See:** open ground, hard 1 m sidestep, missed shots. Around walls, grid-following is expected. Piles shoving into walls feed B01.

**Cause:** A* on walkable **cells**, steer to next cell, not to you. Navmesh would be wrong the moment a block breaks.

**Build:** if a walkable line to the player (or a later waypoint) is clear, steer straight; else keep the grid. Don’t steer through movement-solids. Don’t override a thump/break path. Cap how often we raycast. Dedicated soak — early Path Smoothing builds hung servers; they rewrote it. Too-greedy steer → they skip “break this door.”

Prior art: [Redbeardt Path Smoothing](https://www.nexusmods.com/7daystodie/mods/4110), [GridlessZombies](https://github.com/Djentleman89/GridlessZombies). Steam “Jay’s Pathfind Fix” is **Zomboid**, not 7D2D.

**Test:** straight in a field; 20 zeds no hitch; door still thumped; crawl still works; horde tick ok; no fight with Screamer Alert / VET.

## B05 — Pile-up push-through (blood moon / hatch / bars)

**See:** wall, hatch, or bars still up; enough zeds stack; one (or a crawler) is suddenly **inside** the fortified box, walking and hitting. Common on hatch corridors, vertical bars + blade traps, ladder/scaffold walls. Worse after sledge/turret ragdoll or legs blown off. Players also get stuffed *into* the wall (cousin; same resolver, other body).

Not “they broke the block.” The barrier is intact. Not a lone floor-sink (B01) or a hug-hit through a closed door while they stay outside (B02) — though once inside, their punch still should not damage you.

**Cause:**

1. Same small crawl AABB + wrong-side voxel resolve as B01/B02.
2. **Entity-entity push.** Many colliders into one face; the engine moves someone along the penetration vector. That vector often points *through* the wall, not back into the crowd.
3. **Stack-crawl.** They stand on each other and treat the 1-high gap above a hatch / missing ladder rung as a legal crawl. TFP said 1-block crawl was for vents; piles invent a gap.
4. **Ragdoll / crawler.** Stun, sledge turret, or dismember drops the box further; the next shove wins.

Horde night is the repro: 30+ on one face, every wave.

**Build (v1 = B01/B02, no new physics):** if they’re on **your side** of a wall/door that still exists and they didn’t walk around it, **their punch does no damage** and we **push them back** outside. Don’t kill them. If they came through an **open** hatch or a hole they actually broke, leave them alone.

**Later (only if v1 still leaks on horde night):** reject a position delta that would cross an intact movement-solid. Risk: they jitter against the wall or stop thumping. Cap how hard zed-zed push is near a player-made barrier. Do **not** disable crawl.

**Test:** closed cube wall, 20+ debug-spawned walkers — nobody inside while the wall has HP; they still thump. Hatch with a pole/shutter above (player anti-stack) vs hatch with a 1-high air gap (stack-crawl — decide if we treat that as legal). Bars + blade trap: crawler/ragdoll doesn’t pop through. Open the hatch: they can come in. Player not launched into the void when we snap them back.

**Refs:** [1-block pile / blown through](https://steamcommunity.com/app/251570/discussions/1/3808409963276561032/) · [hatch ragdoll + push](https://steamcommunity.com/app/251570/discussions/0/4676423350584097611/) · [scaffold ladders](https://steamcommunity.com/app/251570/discussions/1/594027376316258957/) · [floor/wall clip](https://community.thefunpimps.com/threads/zs-glitching-through-walls-and-floors.44739/)

## B06 — Face-hit pain anim walks them toward you

**See:** you hit them in the face (spear is the clear case) **before** pain resist is high. They play a pain anim and **close the gap faster than if you had not hit them**. They do **not** punch you during that anim.

Two anims called out:

- Hands on the face, waving side to side
- Legs buckle **forward**

**Not this:** `PainResistPerHit` (vanilla e.g. `0.55` per hit, cap 3, decay `0.2`/s). At **1**, pain no longer slows movement — that is the “they eat the hit and keep coming / can hit with no pain slow” rule. Frantic_Dan: **hitting you with no swing anim** is that meter, not B06.

**Cause (to confirm on decompile):** those pain reacts have **forward root motion** (especially the buckle). A successful face hit therefore **speeds them into you**. Opposite of a useful stagger.

**v1 (toggle):** while those pain reacts play, do not let the anim move them **closer** to the attacker. Keep the flinch. Don’t change PainResistPerHit. Don’t cancel a real sledge knockback away from you.

**Test:** spear to the face, resist still low — they hold their face or buckle, they don’t gain a step. Same hit on the body/legs if those anims don’t step in. After resist is high: vanilla (no pain slow). They don’t sneak a punch *during* the face-hold anim.

**Refs:** player + Frantic_Dan (2026-08-22). Keep separate from “no-anim hit” = PainResist.

## B07 — Your swing hits grass / plant, not the zombie

**See:** field, crop, tall grass, bush, or a planted block between you and them. Your melee connects with the plant. They can still slap you through it.

**Cause:** `treeGrassMaster`, `cropsGrowingMaster`, `cropsHarvestableMaster` (and children) set `Collide="melee"` only. Player `ItemActionMelee` / `ItemActionDynamicMelee` `Voxel.Raycast` stops on `IsCollideMelee`. Vanilla already tries “entity first,” then “blocks” — if the first hit is grass it decides there is no entity. Zombie punch is origin + Range + Sphere — it never asked the plant. Same flag, two rules.

Do **not** strip `Collide` from grass XML. That bit is what harvest / path-clear / plant HP use.

**v1 (toggle):** if **this swing’s ray** already has a living entity in weapon Range, and the only thing in front is a **loose plant** (melee collide, no movement; plants / tall grass / leaves / grass-billboard shapes), hit the entity. Otherwise vanilla — the plant takes the swing. Closed door / hatch / bars / wall / real trunk still stop you. Wood spikes are a trap, not a plant. Same hook on `GetExecuteActionTarget` and the dynamic sweep so the plant does not consume the swing. Do not also damage the plant on a through-hit (one swing, one result). Do not strip XML `Collide`.

| You are doing | Result |
|---|---|
| They are in or behind grass/crops/bush, on this ray, in Range | Hit them |
| Clearing / harvesting, nobody on the ray | Plant is hit (tools, overlay, bonuses unchanged) |
| Looking at the crop at your feet; zombie off to the side | Plant is hit |
| Closed door or trap spikes in front | Still that block |

**Also v1:** grass is **not** cover against *their* punch (same as #1).

**Test:** tall grass / blueberry between you and a walker — they can hit you, and your club hits them. Empty field still harvests. Closed wood door still eats your swing.

## B08 — Hitbox gone while they get up

**See:** they are on the ground (sledge / stun / ragdoll). The moment they start standing, your **swing or shot** goes through empty air for a few frames. Then the box is back. Same empty window for clubs and for guns / bow / crossbow.

**Cause (v3.1 `EModelBase`):** ragdoll start turns **off** the zombie `PhysicsTransform` (players keep theirs). While ragdolled you still have ragdoll bone colliders. Get-up (`DisableRagdoll` → `GetUpChest` / `GetUpBack` / `CrawlerGetUpChest`) switches bones back to “collision” mode, but the walking capsule stays **off** until `Stand` and `ragdollTime >= 0.8` (~0.7 s blend + 0.8 s stand). Melee and hitscan/projectile both look for that box. Stun get-up without ragdoll is a different anim path (`EndStun`) — confirm in play if that one also blanks.

**v1 (toggle, low-risk):** do **not** turn their colliders back on. Leave ragdoll and `PhysicsTransform` as TFP left them. If they are in that get-up / blend window, your **melee ray or ranged shot** (gun / bow / crossbow) passes through chest/pelvis, and no closed door/wall/hatch is in the way, the hit counts. A shot that never crossed the body still misses. Explosions / vomit stay vanilla. Do not snap them while they are still in ragdoll / get-up.

**Don’t (high break risk):** leave the walking capsule on during ragdoll, or force a standing capsule while the mesh is still on the ground.

**Test:** sledge knockdown, swing **and** shoot as they rise — both connect. Misses that never crossed the body still miss. Crawl get-up and animal knockdown don’t launch or lose vents.
