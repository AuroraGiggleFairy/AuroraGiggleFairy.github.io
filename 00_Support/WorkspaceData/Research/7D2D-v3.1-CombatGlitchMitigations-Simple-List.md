# 7D2D combat — now vs what we want

I am aiming to mitigate some of the biggest annoyances and glitches of 7D2D combat. I am not simply shortening zombie reach, and I am not targeting the engine or how the game functions — merely adding mitigation so combat feels more predictable.

1. **Closed door / wall / hatch / bars**  
   **Now:** they punch you through it.  
   **Aim:** if it’s still closed and in the way, that punch does not hit you.

2. **No swing**  
   **Now:** you take a punch even though they never swung.  
   **Aim:** no swing, no punch damage.

3. **You already backed up**  
   **Now:** they start the swing, you step out, you still get hit.  
   **Aim:** if you’re out of reach when it lands, it misses.

4. **Stuck in the floor or wall**  
   **Now:** they’re in the floor, in the wall, or shoved through on horde night — and they keep hitting you.  
   **Aim:** they can’t hit you from in there; after a short wait they get pulled back out.

5. **Hit them in the face**  
   **Now:** they grab their face or buckle, and that flinch walks them closer.  
   **Aim:** they still flinch. They don’t get a free step in.

6. **Grass / plants**  
   **Now:** your swing hits the plant; they still hit you through it.  
   **Aim:** if you were swinging at them, you hit them. Swinging at grass with nobody there still hits the grass.

7. **They get up off the ground**  
   **Now:** for a moment you swing or shoot through empty air.  
   **Aim:** if it goes through their body, melee and guns/bows count.
